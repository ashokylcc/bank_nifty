## 📊 Proven Trading Strategies (Stock/Share Markets)

### 1. **Trend Following Strategy** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
**How it works:**
- Identify strong trends (moving averages, price action)
- Enter in direction of trend
- Exit when trend reverses

**Pros:**
- Works in trending markets
- Can capture large moves
- Simple to understand

**Cons:**
- Fails in sideways markets
- Requires patience
- Can have drawdowns

**Best for:** Stocks with clear trends, swing trading

---

### 2. **Mean Reversion Strategy** ⭐⭐⭐⭐
**How it works:**
- Identify overbought/oversold conditions
- Enter when price deviates from mean
- Exit when price returns to mean

**Pros:**
- Works in range-bound markets
- High win rate (60-70%)
- Quick profits

**Cons:**
- Fails in strong trends
- Requires precise timing
- Can have large losses if trend continues

**Best for:** Range-bound stocks, intraday trading

---

### 3. **Breakout Strategy** ⭐⭐⭐⭐
**How it works:**
- Identify support/resistance levels
- Enter when price breaks above/below
- Exit when momentum fades

**Pros:**
- Captures strong moves
- Clear entry signals
- Good risk-reward

**Cons:**
- Many false breakouts
- Requires volume confirmation
- Can be whipsawed

**Best for:** Volatile stocks, momentum trading

---

### 4. **Momentum Strategy** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
**How it works:**
- Identify stocks with strong momentum
- Enter on pullbacks in uptrend
- Exit when momentum weakens

**Pros:**
- High profit potential
- Works in trending markets
- Can ride big moves

**Cons:**
- Requires strong trends
- Can have large drawdowns
- Needs quick exits

**Best for:** High-momentum stocks, swing trading

---

### 5. **Pairs Trading Strategy** ⭐⭐⭐
**How it works:**
- Find correlated stocks
- Buy underperformer, sell outperformer
- Exit when spread normalizes

**Pros:**
- Market-neutral (less risk)
- Works in all markets
- Lower volatility

**Cons:**
- Requires correlation analysis
- Lower profit potential
- Can have correlation breakdowns

**Best for:** Diversified portfolios, risk-averse traders

---

### 6. **Scalping Strategy** ⭐⭐⭐
**How it works:**
- Make many small trades
- Capture small price movements
- Exit quickly (seconds to minutes)

**Pros:**
- Many opportunities
- Small risk per trade
- Can compound quickly

**Cons:**
- Requires high win rate (70%+)
- High transaction costs
- Stressful (many decisions)

**Best for:** Liquid stocks, experienced traders

---

### 7. **Swing Trading Strategy** ⭐⭐⭐⭐
**How it works:**
- Hold positions for days/weeks
- Capture medium-term moves
- Use technical analysis

**Pros:**
- Less time-intensive
- Can capture larger moves
- Lower transaction costs

**Cons:**
- Requires patience
- Overnight risk
- Needs capital for margin

**Best for:** Part-time traders, medium-term moves

---

### 8. **Value Investing Strategy** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
**How it works:**
- Find undervalued stocks
- Buy and hold long-term
- Exit when overvalued

**Pros:**
- Proven long-term strategy
- Lower stress
- Compounding returns

**Cons:**
- Requires fundamental analysis
- Long holding periods
- Can have long drawdowns

**Best for:** Long-term investors, patient traders

## 🔍 What Makes a Strategy Successful?

### 1. **Clear Entry Rules**
- No ambiguity

### 2. **Clear Exit Rules**
- Profit targets
- Stoploss levels

### 3. **Risk-Reward Ratio**

### 4. **Win Rate**
- Minimum 40% win rate
- Better: 50-60%
- With good risk-reward, 40% can be profitable

### 5. **Consistency**
- Same rules every time
- No emotional decisions
- Systematic approach
